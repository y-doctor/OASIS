E6 vs mTeSR media timecourse — MaGeCK re-analysis

Re-analysis of the E6-vs-mTeSR media fitness screen (IGM run 251013_LH00444_0422_B22YWHGLT4) with MaGeCK on the R2/antiBarcode count matrix. Every figure renders as PNG (click for full size) and is downloadable as PNG or vector SVG.

Methods for this run:

• CPM filter (Day 4 + plasmid only): constructs with CPM<1 in Day4_r1, Day4_r2 or NGS_plasmid were dropped before testing (744/770 constructs, 504/521 ligands kept).

• Per-construct grounding (gene = barcode id, 1 sgRNA/gene), 15 independent MaGeCK tests, each median-normalized and FDR-corrected. E6-vs-mTeSR uses mTeSR as control (+LFC = enriched in E6).

• Gene naming: ligand genes carry _HUMAN (stripped for display); NTC references carry _CONTROL (AMPR/CLUC/GLUC/HALO/MCHERRY/SNAP_CONTROL).

• Hit definition: |LFC z-score| > 2 and FDR < 0.05. Blue = enriched, red = depleted.

• Headline: the FGF family enriches in E6 (autocrine rescue of FGF2/TGFβ-stripped media) — 2→6→18 enriched hits across D7→D9→D14, one-directional; a built-in positive control.

E6 vs mTeSR — the media effect

The headline contrast (mTeSR = control, so +LFC = enriched in E6). LFC z-score (x) vs MaGeCK ligand score (y); each point is a construct. Enriched hits climb 2→6→18 across D7→D9→D14, all one-directional.

FGF-family rescue

E6 (Essential 6) is mTeSR/E8 minus FGF2 and TGFβ. Ligands that supply their own FGF autocrine-rescue the FGF starvation, so the FGF family enriches in E6 over time. The bar panel contrasts the FGF hits against the 6 NTC controls across the timecourse; brackets show the per-day FGF-vs-NTC Welch p-value (D7 0.42, D9 0.003, D14 7.7e-9). Two-way ANOVA: Group p=5.8e-14, Day p=8.2e-16, Group×Day p=6.4e-12. CPM-filtered per-construct data, consistent with the rest of this gallery.

Hit overlap across timepoints

Enriched hits (|Z|>2 & FDR<0.05) shared across Day 7 / Day 9 / Day 14 — one Venn per arm, circles are timepoints. The central lobe is the persistent (all-three-days) set. The E6 and mTeSR arms are each vs their Day 4 baseline; the third is the E6-vs-mTeSR differential.

Replicate reproducibility (QC)

sgRNA LFC of rep1 vs rep2, CPM-filtered survivors (n=744), against two references. vs plasmid (the deep, stable library reference) the reps are highly reproducible (Pearson r 0.91–0.93 at every condition, incl. Day 4). vs the Day 4 mean the common representation signal is subtracted out, leaving only small biological fitness differences, so r is modest early (0.23–0.28) and climbs as real effects emerge (Day 14: E6 0.52, mTeSR 0.40). Both are correct — they measure different things.

CCK8 proliferation validation

Independent CCK8 (WST-8) proliferation assays validating fitness-screen hit ligands against an mCherry control; mean ± SEM, n=3 replicate wells (dots), Student's t-test (uncorrected p + stars), each bar vs the earliest-day mCherry baseline (dashed line). First panel: NRG1/BMP8B/CXCL7/DB133 at Day 3 & 5 (vs mCherry Day 3) — all four significant at both days, DB133 strongest (~2.2×). Second panel: FGF16/NRG2/EGF at Day 1 & 3 (vs mCherry Day 1; this set has no Day 5) — FGF16 is flat at Day 1 then jumps to ~1.6× by Day 3.
