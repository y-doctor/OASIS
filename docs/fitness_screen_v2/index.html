E6 vs mTeSR media timecourse — MaGeCK re-analysis

Re-analysis of the E6-vs-mTeSR media fitness screen (IGM run 251013_LH00444_0422_B22YWHGLT4) with MaGeCK on the R2/antiBarcode count matrix. Every figure renders as PNG (click for full size) and is downloadable as PNG or vector SVG.

Methods for this run:

• CPM filter (Day 4 + plasmid only): constructs with CPM<1 in Day4_r1, Day4_r2 or NGS_plasmid were dropped before testing (744/770 constructs, 504/521 ligands kept).

• Per-construct grounding (gene = barcode id, 1 sgRNA/gene), 15 independent MaGeCK tests, each median-normalized and FDR-corrected. E6-vs-mTeSR uses mTeSR as control (+LFC = enriched in E6).

• Gene naming: ligand genes carry _HUMAN (stripped for display); NTC references carry _CONTROL (AMPR/CLUC/GLUC/HALO/MCHERRY/SNAP_CONTROL).

• Hit definition: |LFC z-score| > 2 and FDR < 0.05. Blue = enriched, red = depleted.

• Headline: the FGF family enriches in E6 (autocrine rescue of FGF2/TGFβ-stripped media) — 2→6→18 enriched hits across D7→D9→D14, one-directional; a built-in positive control.

E6 vs mTeSR — the media effect

The headline contrast (mTeSR = control, so +LFC = enriched in E6). LFC z-score (x) vs MaGeCK ligand score (y); each point is a construct. Enriched hits climb 2→6→18 across D7→D9→D14, all one-directional.

FGF-family rescue

E6 (Essential 6) is mTeSR/E8 minus FGF2 and TGFβ. Ligands that supply their own FGF autocrine-rescue the FGF starvation, so the FGF family enriches in E6 over time. The bar panel contrasts the FGF hits against the 6 NTC controls across the timecourse; brackets show the per-day FGF-vs-NTC Welch p-value (D7 0.42, D9 0.003, D14 7.7e-9). Two-way ANOVA: Group p=5.8e-14, Day p=8.2e-16, Group×Day p=6.4e-12. CPM-filtered per-construct data, consistent with the rest of this gallery.

Replicate reproducibility (QC)

sgRNA LFC of rep1 vs rep2, CPM-filtered survivors (n=744), against two references. vs plasmid (the deep, stable library reference) the reps are highly reproducible (Pearson r 0.91–0.93 at every condition, incl. T-cell RNA-seq validation — ligand vs SNAP at matched timepoints

Bulk RNA-seq validation of two screen hits (CCL13, IL18) against the SNAP control in primary T cells at Day 4 / 8 / 12 (IGM run 260720_LH00444_0550_B23MVY3LT4; STAR → featureCounts → pydeseq2, n=2 per group). Because raw depth ranged 32M–557M pairs across the 18 libraries, every panel in these three sections is computed on BAMs randomly downsampled to 20M mapped pairs (seed 42) so all samples are depth-matched. Result: 0 DEGs at padj<0.05 & |LFC|≥2 in all six ligand-vs-SNAP contrasts. The replicate panels show why this is a genuine null rather than a technical failure: count-level replicate correlation is ~0.997, but the replicates' fold-changes vs SNAP agree only weakly (r 0.19–0.70) because there is no real fold-change for them to agree on. D8 is the least-null timepoint in both arms.

T-cell RNA-seq — within-ligand time course (vs its own Day 4)

Each arm compared to its own Day 4 baseline: SNAP D8/D12 vs SNAP D4, CCL13 D8/D12 vs CCL13 D4, IL18 D8/D12 vs IL18 D4. This is where the dataset's real signal lives — ~1,000–1,700 DEGs per comparison with replicate LFC correlations of 0.91–0.98, which validates the libraries, the counts and the DESeq2 setup, and confirms that the flat ligand-vs-SNAP result above is biology and not a depth or normalisation artefact. About 70% of the D4→D8 DEGs are shared by all three arms, i.e. most of the change is culture/activation rather than ligand-driven. The exception is IL18 at D8 (1,008 up / 698 down, the largest set, with 278 up-only and 268 down-only genes beyond SNAP and CCL13). GO: the shared programme is a coordinated loss of the cytokine/inflammatory and B-cell-receptor signalling signature with ribosome-biogenesis down, and calcium-channel / synapse-organisation terms up by D12.

T-cell RNA-seq — every group vs SNAP Day 4

The same eight groups all referenced to the single SNAP Day 4 baseline, so time and ligand effects sit on one common scale. The two SNAP rows are by construction identical to the within-ligand contrasts above. The pattern is unambiguous: the two same-day contrasts (CCL13 D4, IL18 D4 vs SNAP D4) return 0 DEGs, while every D8 and D12 group returns ~1,000–1,700 DEGs that closely track the SNAP time course — the time axis dominates and the ligands contribute little on top of it.
